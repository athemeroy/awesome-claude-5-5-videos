# Fourteen cases: similar results, different production chains

These **purposefully selected** examples cover all seven production paths in the [168-case audit table](../data/cases.csv). They are not a random sample, a prevalence estimate, a quality ranking, or a controlled model comparison. “Creator says” identifies a reported workflow; a public prompt states a request, not an execution log. The observations come from nine frames of an accessible X preview MP4. Nine frames cannot verify full-motion quality, audio, factual accuracy, hidden tool calls, or the highest-resolution master. See the [field definitions](case-index-guide.zh-CN.md) and [method](methodology.zh-CN.md).

The 14 small stills below each show one frame from the corresponding accessible X preview. Click an image to open the creator's original post. They help identify the visible style; they do not assess full-motion, audio, or master quality. Rights to the source frames remain with their creators.
