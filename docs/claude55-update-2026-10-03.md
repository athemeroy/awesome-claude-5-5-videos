# October 3: human history, art history and more Claude videos

[中文](claude55-update-2026-10-03.zh-CN.md) · [Home](../README.md) · [October 2](claude55-update-2026-10-02.md)

This update adds **157 annotated source posts**, including reported Fable work, Opus/Sonnet work, comparisons and version variants. Each entry links to the publisher’s original post. This is not a count of independent films or verified model runs. The separate [candidate audit](../data/claude55-candidate-audit-2026-10-03.csv) retains 602 post, quote and thread IDs.

The discovery starts with [WangYeruo’s quote](https://x.com/WangYeruo/status/2106212447870947409), through [Tz](https://x.com/Tz_2022/status/2106129476513878112), to [imjustnewatai’s human-progress film](https://x.com/imjustnewatai/status/2106081142143168580). This chain contributes one work, not three.

**Start with the films.** A one-day clock organizes human progress; the art-history film carries an artist and cat through changing styles; the scale film joins microscopic structures, a person and galaxies with one visual idea. These are observations of sampled frames, not model rankings.

We used ffprobe/ffmpeg to probe and sample nine frames from **15 previews**, then inspected the contact sheets. 14 correspond to entries below; one is an unaccepted MV claim. Review covers sampled frames and audio-track presence, not full continuous playback or independent score attribution. Other entries are source-only. Reported Fable 5.5 backend identities remain unverified.

## Creator replies that change the interpretation

- [The three-body creator clarifies](https://x.com/zAdrielsan/status/2105867394296250790) that the first four formal proofs covered controls. [Opus was subsequently asked for physics checks](https://x.com/zAdrielsan/status/2105872131250958368), followed by [another proof update](https://x.com/zAdrielsan/status/2105884574303535565). This is not a one-shot proof of a general three-body solution.
- [The public moonwalk brief](https://x.com/noclipepe/status/2106154090556199006) includes an image reference and detailed character, motion and environment requirements. The retrieved recording is 33.62 seconds; it is not evidence of a context-free one-liner.
- [The kinetic-typography creator](https://x.com/atomtanstudio/status/2106135196483608745) supplied the song and lyrics. Existing music must not become an “original Fable score.”
- [The “12 hours Fable MV” post](https://x.com/synthshareai/status/2106195060140446028) samples show a mascot over existing live-action music-video footage. They do not support the original-MV claim; it is excluded from the new directory.

## Search coverage and deduplication

Queries cover exact Fable labels, date windows, English animation/music/film terms, Chinese video/animation/3D terms, compact version spellings, Opus, Sonnet and two creators. Date filters are requested conditions only: some returned creation dates fall outside their windows, so we cannot claim complete daily coverage. One creator search returned HTTP 429 and one broad dated query timed out. Both failures are preserved without automatic retries.

Deduplication uses post IDs and shared X video-object IDs. It is not a corpus-wide perceptual comparison of re-encodings, recordings or variants, so source rows must not be reported as independent-film totals. Quotes retain earlier sources. Unestablished creators, commentary, tutorials and promotional noise stay in the candidate audit rather than becoming claimed original works.

This is not an exhaustive census of X or the web. Subsequent posts, unindexed/private/deleted sources and results beyond caps remain outside verified coverage. The September 26 statistics, 168 Hypit-reviewed cases and existing chart denominators remain frozen.
